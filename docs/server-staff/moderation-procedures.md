## The escalation ladder

Follow in order. Skipping steps needs a reason (noted in the incident log).

1. **Verbal warning** — friendly, in-channel or DM. Most first offenses end here.
2. **Official warning** — logged warning via the bot. Member is told what happens next time.
3. **Timeout / mute** — > **TODO:** set durations, e.g. 1 hour → 24 hours.
4. **Kick** — for repeat offenses or deliberate disruption. They can rejoin.
5. **Ban** — for serious or repeated violations. Permanent unless appealed.

## Zero-tolerance (straight to ban)

- Illegal content of any kind
- Targeted harassment or hate speech
- Sharing someone's personal info (doxxing)
- Predatory behavior — especially critical in a little space community
- Raiding or ban evasion

Act immediately, then write it up.

## How to handle common situations

**Spam / flooding**
Warn → delete messages → timeout if it continues.

**Heated argument**
Split them up: ask both to cool down in DMs, timeout if needed. Don't take sides publicly.

**NSFW where it doesn't belong**
Delete, warn, remind them where that content goes. Repeat = timeout.

**Someone reports a member**
Thank them, look into it quietly, don't announce the investigation. Update the reporter when resolved ("handled, thanks for flagging").

## After any action

1. Log it (who, what, when, why).
2. If it was significant, drop a note in the staff channel.
3. Never argue about mod decisions in public channels — "decisions are final, appeal via ticket" and move on.

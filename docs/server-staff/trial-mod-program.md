## Expectations

- Ask questions. Nobody expects you to know everything — that's what the trial is for.
- Log everything you do so your mentor can review it.
- Take feedback gracefully. Corrections aren't criticism; they're how you grow into the role.

## For mentors

- Review your trial mod's actions daily at first.
- Explain the *why* behind procedures, not just the *what*.
- Give honest feedback in the trial review — promoting someone who isn't ready helps nobody.

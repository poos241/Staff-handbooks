# Moderation Procedures & Case Handling

While each situation can be unique, we follow general procedures to keep things consistent, fair, and well-documented. Discretion is always allowed and encouraged — provided your actions don't deviate significantly from established expectations.

:::tip A guide, not a rigid rulebook

Treat these steps as a guide. Use your judgment when needed, and always act with fairness, clarity, and the community's safety in mind.
:::

:::info Visual reference

An image version of the moderation guidelines is pinned in the staff channels and can be used as a helpful quick reference alongside this page.
:::

## Timeout Procedure

When issuing a timeout:

1. **Use the appropriate command** with the recommended duration.
2. **Immediately open a ticket** with the timed-out user.
3. **Inform them** of the timeout, the reason, and when the timeout will end.
4. **Keep the ticket open** for the duration of the timeout.
5. Once the timeout ends:
   - Inform the user that it has ended.
   - Request an acknowledgment of the action and understanding of the rules going forward.
6. If the user does not acknowledge, follow the escalation procedure below.

## Ticket Acknowledgment & Escalation

Whenever you issue a warning or timeout, you must:

1. **Open a ticket** with the user.
2. **Clearly state** what action is being taken (e.g. *"You are receiving an informal warning for XYZ"*).
3. **Ask the user to acknowledge** the message.

If the user does not respond:

- **Wait 2 days**, then ping them once in the ticket as a follow-up.
- If they still don't respond **within 24 hours** of that ping:
  - At your discretion, you may **escalate their punishment to the next appropriate category** (e.g. informal warning → formal warning, or timeout → longer timeout/ban).
  - Clearly state this in the ticket before closing it.

:::warning Always close with a reason

Always provide a clear reason for closing any ticket. This maintains transparency and makes case histories easier to track.
:::

# New Moderator Onboarding Checklist

import Checklist from '@site/src/components/Checklist';
import Link from '@docusaurus/Link';

Welcome aboard! Work through these checklists in your first week — your progress saves automatically in your browser. Ask in the staff chat any time you're unsure.

> **Note for leads:** adjust names, channels, and permission steps below to match the server as it changes.

## Day 1 — Read and get set up

<Checklist
  id="onboarding-day1"
  items={[
    <>Read the <Link to="/moderation/">Moderation Handbook intro</Link>, including the privacy warning</>,
    <>Read <Link to="/moderation/rules-and-guidelines">Rules and Guidelines</Link> — all four sections</>,
    <>Read <Link to="/moderation/mod-commands">Mod Commands</Link> and note which commands <em>you</em> are granted</>,
    'Confirm you can see and post in #mod-commands',
    'Confirm you can view tickets and #evidence-for-logs',
    'Introduce yourself in the staff chat',
  ]}
/>

## Days 2–4 — Learn the ropes

<Checklist
  id="onboarding-days24"
  items={[
    <>Read <Link to="/moderation/procedures">Moderation Procedures</Link>, including the punishment ladder</>,
    <>Read <Link to="/moderation/informing-members">Informing Members of Punishments</Link></>,
    'Look up a test user with /modlogs to see how case history reads',
    "Shadow a senior moderator on at least one live ticket — watch, don't act",
    <>Bookmark the <Link to="/moderation/quick-reference">Quick Reference</Link> cheat sheet</>,
  ]}
/>

## Days 5–7 — First supervised actions

<Checklist
  id="onboarding-days57"
  items={[
    'Handle your first simple case (e.g. off-topic cleanup, duplicate intro) with a senior mod available',
    'Open, work, and close a practice ticket following the ticket rules',
    'Log evidence correctly: screenshots in #evidence-for-logs with user IDs',
    'Get feedback from your supervising moderator',
  ]}
/>

## Ongoing — good habits

<Checklist
  id="onboarding-ongoing"
  items={[
    <>Check <Link to="/updates">Handbook Updates</Link> whenever you're notified of a change</>,
    "Only moderate in a clear headspace — step back and hand off when you're not",
    'Stay neutral: hand off cases involving friends (or people you dislike)',
    'When in doubt, ask in staff chat before acting — especially on punishments',
  ]}
/>

:::tip You're done when…

…every checklist above reads **completed** *and* a senior moderator signs off. There's no rush — thorough beats fast.
:::

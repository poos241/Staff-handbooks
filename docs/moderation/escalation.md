1. **Trial Mod** — new moderators in their evaluation period
2. **Moderator** — full moderators

## How to escalate well

- **Summarize first:** who, what happened, what you've already done, and what you need.
- **Link the evidence:** ticket link, `#evidence-for-logs` screenshots, case numbers.
- **Don't keep acting** on the case while waiting — hand it off cleanly.
- **Tell the member** what's happening: "I'm bringing in a senior moderator to review this."

:::warning Never punish out of frustration

If a case is getting to you, escalate it and step away. Handing off is always the right call — it's not giving up.
:::

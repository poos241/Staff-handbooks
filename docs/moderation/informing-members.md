# Informing Members of Punishments

import DiscordMessage from '@site/src/components/DiscordMessage';

At Little Haven, we value privacy, safety, and community trust. Moderators play an important role in handling rule-breaking and ensuring punishments are communicated in a way that protects all members.

## 1. Keep Punishments Private

Details about warnings, mutes, or other punishments should remain between staff and the member involved. Other members don't need to know what actions were taken — sharing this can lead to gossip, bullying, or members feeling excluded.

If a member asks about someone else's punishment, politely explain that staff don't share private matters.

<DiscordMessage user="Haven Mod" time="Today at 3:42 PM">

"We don't share details of punishments to protect everyone's privacy. Thanks for understanding."

</DiscordMessage>

## 2. Announcing Bans

You **may** announce bans so members know why someone has disappeared. Use your judgment about how much detail to give:

- **Sensitive reason** (e.g. assault, self-harm, harassment that may trigger another member) → keep the explanation **minimal**.
- **Clear, community-relevant reason** (e.g. repeated rule-breaking, hate speech, trolling) → you may state it **plainly**.

**Appropriate:**

<DiscordMessage user="Haven Mod" time="Today at 5:10 PM">

User X has been banned due to repeated rule violations.

</DiscordMessage>

**Too much detail — don't do this:**

<DiscordMessage user="Haven Mod" time="Today at 5:10 PM" color="#ed4245">

User X was banned for harassing a survivor of assault.

</DiscordMessage>

## 3. Balancing Transparency & Safety

- Share only what the community needs to know to feel safe.
- Avoid framing punishments in ways that invite members to take sides or discuss private conflicts.
- If an incident may affect the community's sense of safety (e.g. someone was predatory), a general warning without naming names may be posted.

<DiscordMessage user="Haven Mod" time="Yesterday at 8:03 PM">

We want to remind everyone to report any behavior that makes you uncomfortable. Safety is our top priority.

</DiscordMessage>

## 4. Respecting Victims and Vulnerable Members

- **Never reveal details** that could identify or retraumatize a victim.
- When in doubt, keep your announcement vague but firm.
- You can acknowledge that action was taken without disclosing specifics.

## 5. Internal Documentation

Staff should keep clear internal records of punishments, including **reasons, dates, and actions taken**. This ensures consistency and accountability within the team, even when members aren't told the details.

:::tip Log your evidence

- Take screenshots of interactions that led to punishment and put them in `#evidence-for-logs` with the user IDs of everyone involved.
- Put the user IDs of banned users in the tickets that reported them, for logging purposes.
:::

## 6. Unified Communication

- Moderators should **not contradict each other publicly**.
- If you're unsure how to phrase something, **ask in the staff chat** before posting.
- Present punishments as a **team decision**, not a personal one.

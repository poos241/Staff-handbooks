# Moderation Handbook

:::danger Do not share this handbook

Do not share this handbook with anyone outside of the Little Haven moderation team or Little Haven management. Doing so may result in a formal warning, sanction, or immediate removal from the moderation team. This handbook is kept private so that security within the moderation team stays as high as possible. If you suspect it has been shared, contact the Head Moderator immediately.

:::

## Introduction

As a moderator, you're expected to uphold the server's core principles and rules, and ensure the server is a safe and welcoming place for newcomers and veteran members alike. You're not just enforcers — you're also support staff who help the community by providing necessary or requested information about the server, help on specific topics, advice and guidance to all members, and showing a kind and caring face for the moderation team.

This handbook covers everything you'll need to know as a moderator:

- 📜 **[Rules and Guidelines](./rules-and-guidelines)** — the rules you follow as a moderator, including ticket handling
- ⌨️ **[Mod Commands](./mod-commands)** — command rules and the full command reference
- 🔨 **[Moderation Procedures](./procedures)** — timeout procedure, ticket acknowledgment and escalation
- 🔒 **[Informing Members of Punishments](./informing-members)** — how to communicate actions privately and publicly
- ⚡ **[Quick Reference](./quick-reference)** — the need-to-know notes and a cheat sheet

With all that said — **welcome to the moderation team!**

:::info Keeping this handbook current

This handbook is updated when and where necessary. All moderators will receive a clear and accurate notice whenever changes occur.

:::

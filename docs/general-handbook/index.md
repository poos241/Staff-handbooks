# General Handbook

Welcome to the staff team! This handbook covers what **every** staff member needs to know, no matter your role.

## How to use these handbooks

- **General Handbook** (this one) — code of conduct, role overview, and how we communicate. Everyone reads this.
- **Server Staff Handbook** — for moderators and trial mods: duties and moderation procedures.
- **Event Staff Handbook** — for event hosts and event security: running and protecting events.
- **Server IT Handbook** — for the tech team: bots, tools, and troubleshooting.

## First steps for new staff

1. Read this General Handbook fully.
2. Read the handbook for your specific role.
3. Introduce yourself in the staff channel.
4. Shadow an experienced team member for your first week — ask questions freely.

## A note on our community

We are a little space community on Discord and VRChat — a comfort-focused, playful space. Many members are here to relax and feel safe. As staff, your #1 job is protecting that feeling. Be warm, be patient, and remember that for some members this is their soft place to land.

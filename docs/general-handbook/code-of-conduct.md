# Code of Conduct

Applies to all staff, all roles, on Discord and in VRChat.

## The basics

1. **Be kind first.** We're a comfort community. Lead with warmth, even when enforcing rules.
2. **No power trips.** Your role is a responsibility, not a status symbol. Never use staff powers to win arguments or settle scores.
3. **Keep it professional in public.** Members watch how staff behave — you're setting the tone.
4. **Respect privacy.** What's shared in staff channels stays in staff channels. Never share DMs, tickets, or internal discussions with members.
5. **No favoritism.** Enforce rules evenly, whether someone is your friend or a stranger.
6. **Stay in your lane.** Mods handle Discord, hosts run events, security handles VRChat instances, IT handles tech. If something's outside your role, escalate instead of improvising.

## Escalation — who to ping when

Follow this order. Don't skip levels unless it's urgent.

1. **Your team lead** (head mod, lead host, IT lead).
2. **Head mod / admin** for anything crossing teams.
3. **Community owner** for emergencies, staff issues, or PR nightmares.

**Urgent = act now, explain later.** Active raid, someone sharing illegal content, a member in immediate danger — handle it first, write it up after.

## Writing incident notes

When something notable happens, drop a short note in the staff channel:

- What happened (facts only, no editorializing)
- Who was involved (usernames/IDs)
- What you did
- Timestamp

Clear notes keep everyone aligned and protect you if a decision gets questioned later.
